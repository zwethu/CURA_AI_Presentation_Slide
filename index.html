Who is the medical audit?

Every claim passes through one desk at the end of the line.

Step 1 Patient Arrives at OPD, is examined and treated
Step 2 Doctor Records diagnosis and treatment in the chart
Step 3 Medical audit Reads the record, analyses it, assigns ICD-10 and ICD-9-CM codes

01 Coding knowledge & experience ICD-10 and ICD-9-CM conventions, sequencing rules, and the exceptions learned on the job
02 Medical knowledge Reading a clinical note well enough to know what was actually diagnosed and treated
03 Understanding healthcare benefits How each scheme reimburses, and what a claim needs in order to hold up
